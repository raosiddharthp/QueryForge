§2 Standard Payment Terms Glossary
Net-30: payment due 30 days after invoice date. Net-60: payment due 60 days after invoice date.
Net-90: payment due 90 days after invoice date, classified as non-standard per procurement policy.
Upfront deposit: a percentage of contract value paid before work begins.
