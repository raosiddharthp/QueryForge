§1 Purpose
This document defines the procurement approval authority matrix for Acme Corp finance operations.

§2 Standard Approval Thresholds
Purchases under $10,000 require manager sign-off only. Purchases between $10,000 and $50,000
require Director-level approval. Purchases over $50,000 require VP Finance approval and a
signed procurement review form.

§3 Vendor Contract Approval Threshold
Any vendor contract exceeding $50,000 in total committed value must be routed to VP Finance
regardless of the payment structure. This applies whether the contract is a single payment,
milestone-based, or subscription billing.

§4 Escalation for Non-Standard Terms
§4.1 Definition
Non-standard payment terms include net-90 or longer payment windows, upfront deposits exceeding
30% of contract value, or any currency other than USD.

§4.2 Non-standard payment terms require additional Finance sign-off beyond the standard threshold
approval in §3. The requesting manager must attach a risk memo describing why standard net-30
terms were not accepted by the vendor.

§5 Authority Matrix Summary
Manager: up to $10,000. Director: $10,000-$50,000. VP Finance: over $50,000 or any non-standard
payment terms regardless of contract value.
